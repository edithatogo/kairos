# Supplemental statistical readback binding

C4.4 diagnostics carry actual strata to bind each opaque group to exactly one W1/KS pair. source_manifest.json adds canonical metric_groups (group,strata) from the producer input. They must be unique, sorted and exactly reconcile the join supplement, including empty declared groups. Raw source-clock/window selection and exact Fraction supports independently reconstruct eligible sample and tie counts; rejected/unverified ties remain null. Overlapping raw diagnostics derive warnings. No inference or survival correction is claimed.

The C4.3 reader validates present supplements. --require-statistical-diagnostics additionally rejects absent diagnostics and declared groups recursively in every framing. Historical C4.3 artifacts remain readable without this flag. Native-owner current and both-feature floor jobs use the required flag. Self-tests plus actual copied-output mutations reject forged counts/warnings/strata, Boolean counts, missing/empty diagnostics and missing declarations. Supplemental native fixtures permit standalone exact JSON readback through statistical_diagnostics.py; baseline C4.3 physical readback remains separate.

## Complete cohort provenance binding

Strict statistical readback requires every source run to retain nonempty `dataset_id`, `run_id`, `mapping_version`, `seed_schedule_id`, `seed_map_ref`, `parameter_hash`, `seed_contract_version` and `candidate_id`. Parameter hashes are lowercase SHA-256. W1 and KS records must share endpoint, strata, window and full provenance; their required provenance fields must resolve exactly to one source run, and the group strata candidate must match that run. Missing fields are not wildcards. Invalid/unverified metrics retain zero contributing sample counts and null tie counts while raw coverage diagnostics remain visible.

Declared metric groups are sorted by group identity. The producer rejects duplicate canonical strata, including object-key permutations; the independent reader also rejects ambiguous mappings. Current and both-feature floor CI retain and independently validate actual supplemental tied/overlapping and empty/insufficient producer cases. Historical C4.3 artifacts remain readable without the new strict statistical requirement.
