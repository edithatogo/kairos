## Research readiness checklist

- Does KairoECS have non-trivial research functionality?
- Is there a clear statement of need?
- Are there docs sufficient for new users?
- Are there tests and examples?
- Is there a citation route?
- Are benchmarks reproducible?
- Are all model zoo examples licensed and sourced clearly?
