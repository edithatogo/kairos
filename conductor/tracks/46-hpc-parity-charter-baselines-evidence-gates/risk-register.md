# Track 46 Risk Register

Severity scale: Low (1-2), Medium (3-4), High (5-6), Critical (7-10).

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Scaffold evidence is mistaken for live proof | False parity claim | Gate `Done` on live evidence manifests |
| Baseline comparison is too vague | Unverifiable SOTA claim | Name external runtime, capability, command, and artifact |
| Hardware evidence cannot be reproduced | Benchmark dispute | Require hardware, topology, toolchain, and checksum fields |
| Downstream tracks use incompatible evidence formats | Review friction | Provide one shared manifest contract |
| Claim scans miss registry text | Release overclaim | Include README, docs, packaging, and release-note surfaces |
