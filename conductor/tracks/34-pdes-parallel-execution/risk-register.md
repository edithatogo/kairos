# Risk Register: Track 34 PDES & Parallel Execution

Severity scale: Likelihood 1-5 x Impact 1-5. Low 1-4, Medium 5-9, High 10-16, Critical 17-25.

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Severity | Mitigation | Owner | Escalation trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PDES determinism loss — final state differs from sequential scheduler | 3 | 5 | 15 | Sequential parity test in CI gates; compare component state and entity graph after every PDES run; run Track 12 conformance suite under PDES mode | pdes-agent | Sequential parity test fails on any accepted workload |
| Rollback overhead for Time Warp — optimistic PDES underperforms conservative | 4 | 3 | 12 | Keep Time Warp as research spike only; do not productionize without measurement; compare rollback rate against event density | pdes-agent | Time Warp spike shows rollback rate > 20% for representative workloads |
| LP boundary tuning — poor partition quality limits speedup | 3 | 3 | 9 | Document partitioning best practices; provide diagnostic tools (inter-LP event ratio); benchmark with multiple partition strategies | pdes-agent | Speedup < 1.0x (slower than sequential) for any LP count on representative workloads |
| Lookahead computation complexity — accurate lookahead is domain-specific and hard to compute automatically | 3 | 4 | 12 | Require user-provided lookahead per LP; provide safe default (0) with documented tradeoffs; benchmark sensitivity to lookahead values | pdes-agent | User-provided lookahead produces incorrect results (events arrive "in the past") |
| Deadlock in conservative PDES — null-message protocol may deadlock with zero-lookahead cycles | 2 | 5 | 10 | CMB null-message protocol with non-zero lookahead requirement; deadlock detection with timeout; fallback to sequential mode on deadlock | pdes-agent | Deadlock stress test fails; simulation hangs with zero event progress |
| Runtime evidence drift between MSVC and GNU toolchains | 2 | 3 | 6 | Keep `cargo check --tests` green and run the focused runtime gate through `stable-x86_64-pc-windows-gnu`; rerun on MSVC once local linker resolution is fixed | pdes-agent | GNU runtime gate fails or MSVC rerun exposes behavior not covered by the GNU-toolchain tests |
| Thread scaling overhead — synchronization costs dominate at higher LP counts | 3 | 3 | 9 | Benchmark LP counts from 2 to 32; identify diminishing returns point; document recommended max LP count per core count | pdes-agent | Speedup ceiling reached before 8 cores (e.g., 4x at 4 cores but only 4.5x at 8) |
| Cargo feature flag interaction — PDES code accidentally depends on sequential-only internals | 2 | 4 | 8 | Isolate PDES in separate crate `kairo-ecs-pdes`; CI builds with `--features pdes` and without; no `pub(crate)` visibility across crate boundaries | pdes-agent | Build fails with `--features pdes` on stable toolchain |
