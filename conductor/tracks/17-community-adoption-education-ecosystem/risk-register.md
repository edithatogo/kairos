# Risk Register: Track 17 Community Adoption, Education & Ecosystem

| Risk | L | I | Sev | Mitigation | Owner | Escalation trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contributor onboarding friction | 4 | 4 | 16 | Good-first-issue labels, CONTRIBUTING.md with step-by-step setup, local-dev quickstart | community-agent | >50% of first-time contributors abandon PR within 24hr |
| Discussion/spam moderation burden | 4 | 4 | 16 | Define moderation guidelines, auto-close stale issues, designate moderator rotation | community-agent | Unmoderated spam persists >48hr on any channel |
| Community promises outpace implementation | 4 | 4 | 16 | Mark maturity badges on every feature; require working conformance fixture before public claim | docs-agent | Any public claim for a feature missing a conformance fixture |
| Example code drift | 4 | 4 | 16 | CI-tested example compilation; pin example versions to the release they target | ci-agent | Example compilation CI fails |
| Ecosystem fragmentation | 3 | 3 | 9 | Shared contributor covenant, cross-ecosystem triage, and unified discussion venue | community-agent | Any ecosystem diverges from shared covenant or uses separate discussion venue |
